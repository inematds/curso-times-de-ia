Quick Wins (Week 1)

  1. Call card issuer X and request a rate reduction (script provided below)
  2. Set up automatic payments for all minimums (avoid late fees + small APR discount)
  3. Move the extra $X to the highest-priority debt this week
  4. Open a 0% balance transfer card application at [bank]
  5. Renegotiate vehicle insurance to free up $X/month for debt

Fee Reduction Call Script

"Hello, I’ve been a customer for X years. I’d like to request a lower APR on my account. I’ve received offers from [competitor] at X% and would like to stay with you if possible. Can you lower my rate?"

Success rate: ~30-40%. Even a 2% reduction on $5K saves $100+/year.

After Payoff — Redirect Payments to Build Wealth

In month XX, once you're debt-free, you'll have $X,XXX/month freed up. Allocation plan:

  • $X for an emergency fund (up to 6 months of expenses)
  • $X for retirement (401k match max → Roth → 401k)
  • $X for a taxable brokerage account
  • $X for the next goal (home, business, family)

Risks and Points to Watch

  • Credit card debt buildup (close 0 cards, lower limits)
  • Expiration date of the 0% promotional APR (mark your calendar 60 days in advance)
  • Income interruption (maintain an emergency fund of at least 1 month)
  • Co-signers / joint debts in divorce/separation scenarios

Behavioral Anchors

  • Track on a visible chart (debt thermometer)
  • Celebrate each payoff (small reward, NO new debt)
  • Inform the person in charge
  • 5-minute weekly check
