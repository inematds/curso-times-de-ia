WARNING: For educational/informational purposes only. This does not constitute financial advice. This is not a recommendation to buy any specific security.

Objective

Help the user build or refine an investment portfolio using proven, evidence-based selection criteria. The output is a model portfolio with specific ticker examples, allocation weights, and expected behavior—not a buy/sell recommendation.

When to Trigger

  • The user types /finance screen <estratégia> or /finance screen (and then requests the strategy)
  • The user asks "what should I invest in?", "build a portfolio for me", "which ETFs for X?", "low-cost index fund portfolio"
  • The user wants to add diversification to existing assets

Predefined Criteria

1. Dividend Growth Criterion

Objective: Companies with growing dividends, sustainable payout ratios, and durable competitive advantages.

Criteria:

  • 10+ consecutive years of dividend increases (Dividend Aristocrats / Achievers)
  • Payout ratio < 60%
  • Free cash flow growth > 5%/year
  • Dividend yield 2-5%
  • Debt/Equity < 1.5

Representative Assets:

  • JNJ, PG, KO, PEP, MMM (Dividend Aristocrats)
  • VIG (Vanguard Dividend Appreciation ETF, 0.06% expense ratio)
  • SCHD (Schwab US Dividend Equity, 0,06%)
  • DGRO (iShares Core Dividend Growth, 0,08%)

Expected: Annualized total return of 8–10%, yield of 2–3%, lower volatility than the S&P 500.

2. Three-Fund Index Portfolio (Bogleheads)

Objective: Maximum diversification, lower cost, a “set it and forget it” strategy.

Allocation (age-based):

  • U.S. Total Stock Market: (110 - age)% * 0.6
  • Total International Stocks: (110 - age)% * 0.4
  • U.S. Total Bond Market: age%

Example for age 35: 45% U.S. Stocks / 30% International / 25% Bonds

Representative Assets:

  • VTI (Vanguard Total Stock, 0,03%) or FSKAX
  • VXUS (Vanguard Total International, 0,07%) or FTIHX
  • BND (Vanguard Total Bond, 0.03%) or FXNAX

Expected: Annualized real return of 7–9%, broad market exposure, near-zero idiosyncratic risk.

3. Bond Allocation Criteria

Objective: Stability, income, downside protection against stock declines.

Sub-allocation:

  • U.S. Total Bond Market (intermediate): 50%
  • Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS): 20%
  • Short-Term Treasury Bonds (cash cushion): 15%
  • High-Yield Corporate Bonds (small allocation, optional): 10%
  • International Bonds (USD-hedged): 5%

Representative Assets:

  • BND, AGG (total bond market)
  • VTIP, SCHP (TIPS)
  • VGSH, SHY (Short-term Treasury)
  • JNK, HYG (high yield — flag as higher risk)
  • BNDX (international bonds, USD-hedged)

Expected: Annualized return of 3–5%, low correlation with stocks, downside protection.

4. Real Estate (REITs)

Objective: Exposure to real estate without owning properties; inflation protection; income.

Allocation: Limit REIT exposure to 5-15% of the portfolio.

Representative Assets:

  • VNQ (Vanguard Real Estate, 0.12%)
  • SCHH (Schwab US REIT, 0,07%)
  • REET (iShares Global REIT, 0,14%)
  • O (Realty Income — individual stock, monthly dividend)

Subsectors to consider:

  • Residential, industrial (data centers, logistics), healthcare, self-storage

Expected: Return of 7–9%, yield of 3–4%, sensitive to interest rates.

5. International Diversification

Objective: Reduce home-country bias; capture global growth.

Allocation:

  • Developed International (Europe, Japan, Asia-Pacific): 60% of the international allocation
  • Emerging Markets: 30%
  • International Small-Cap: 10%

Representative Assets:

  • VEA, IXUS (Developed International)
  • VWO, IEMG (Emerging Markets)
  • VSS (International Small Caps)

General rule: 30-40% of the stock allocation in international stocks.

Expected: Return of 6–9%, higher volatility due to currency exposure + emerging markets.

6. ESG / Sustainable

Objective: Environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criterion; align the portfolio with your values.

Representative Assets:

  • ESGV (Vanguard ESG US Stock, 0,09%)
  • VSGX (Vanguard ESG International, 0,12%)
  • ESGD (iShares ESG Developed Markets, 0,20%)
  • SUSL (iShares MSCI USA ESG Leaders, 0,15%)

Notes to flag:

  • ESG definitions vary; check the fund’s methodology
  • Some ESG funds have lower energy exposure and may underperform during energy rallies
  • Higher management fees than common index funds

Expected: Returns close to the broad market index, minus 0.1-0.3%/year; tracking error.

Custom Criterion

If the user specifies custom criteria, accept inputs such as:

  • Sector preference (technology, healthcare, energy, etc.)
  • Geographic Focus (U.S. only, ex-U.S., emerging markets)
  • Factor tilts (value, growth, momentum, quality, small-cap)
  • Yield target (e.g., "3%+ dividend yield")
  • Cap the expense ratio (e.g., “below 0.20%”)
  • Risk tolerance (conservative / moderate / aggressive)
  • ESG Requirements

Combine with relevant predefined criteria and provide a personalized allocation.

Required Inputs

  1. Strategy choice (one of the 6 above, or "custom")
  2. Investable value (lump sum or monthly contribution)
  3. Time horizon (years until needed)
  4. Risk tolerance (scale of 1–10 or conservative/moderate/aggressive)
  5. Age (for age-based allocations)
  6. Account Type (taxable / IRA / Roth / 401k — affects tax-efficient choices)
  7. Existing assets (to avoid overlap and identify concentration)

Output Format

- Sinalizar explicitamente quaisquer veículos de alto custo ou alto risco
- Observar que backtests não preveem o futuro
- Enfatizar a disciplina comportamental em vez do alfa de seleção
