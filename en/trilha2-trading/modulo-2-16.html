Rules

  1. ALWAYS use WebSearch for current pricing and metrics data — never use outdated or fabricated data
  2. ALWAYS sort the watchlist by Quick Score in descending order
  3. ALWAYS check alert conditions during each update
  4. ALWAYS preserve the user’s notes and tags when reclassifying
  5. ALWAYS show score changes compared with the previous assessment
  6. ALWAYS include the disclaimer at the top and in the footer
  7. NEVER automatically remove stocks from the watchlist—only the user can remove them
  8. If a stock scores below 30 in two consecutive re-rankings, suggest removing it, but don’t remove it automatically
  9. When adding actions, confirm that the ticker symbol is valid before scoring
  10. Limit the watchlist to a maximum of 30 stocks — if it’s at capacity, suggest removing the lowest-scoring one
  11. Flag any stock that has been on the watchlist for more than 30 days without improvement
  12. When displaying them, visually highlight the top 3 stocks (they have the highest priority)

Error Handling

  • Invalid ticker: "[TICKER] could not be found. Please check the symbol."
  • Watchlist not found: "No watchlist found. Creating a new one. Add stocks with /trade watchlist add <tickers>."
  • Duplicate addition: "[TICKER] is already on your watchlist (current score: XX/100)."
  • Removal Not Found: "[TICKER] is not on your watchlist."
  • Full watchlist: "The watchlist contains 30 stocks (maximum). Remove a stock first with /trade watchlist remove <ticker>."
