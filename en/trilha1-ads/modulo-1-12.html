Skill Purpose

Build 5 to 7 highly detailed audience personas from a business URL. Each persona goes far beyond basic demographics—it maps psychographic profiles, buying triggers, objections, content consumption habits, platform presence, and targeting parameters ready to use on Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok, and Pinterest. Includes a persona relevance score (1-5) and a negative audience section defining who should NOT be targeted. Produces a single copy-and-paste-ready deliverable that an ad buyer can use immediately to build campaigns.

When to Use

  • The user runs /ads audience <url>
  • The user asks to build audience personas, customer profiles, or targeting research
  • Called as a subagent from /ads strategy (the main orchestrator)
  • The user wants to know "who should I target?" for a business
  • The user needs platform-specific targeting parameters for campaign setup

Entry Requirements

  • Required: A business URL to analyze
  • Optional: Industry context, existing customer data, geographic focus, budget range

How to Run

Stage 2: Industry Audience Intelligence

Based on the detected industry, get standard audience benchmarks:

SaaS/Software:

  • Decision-makers: CTOs, VPs of Engineering, Product Managers, IT Directors
  • Influencers: Individual contributors who discover tools
  • Budget holders: CFOs, COOs, department heads
  • Research behavior: G2 reviews, Product Hunt, Reddit, comparison articles

E-commerce:

  • Impulse buyers vs. researchers
  • Price-sensitive vs. quality-focused segments
  • Brand loyalists vs. deal seekers
  • Social commerce behavior: Instagram Shops, TikTok Shop, Pinterest

Local Services:

  • Buyers with urgent/emergency needs
  • Buyers making planned/purpose-driven purchases
  • Referral-driven customers
  • Neighborhood/community-focused segments

Agency/Professional Services:

  • Decision timeline: 30–90-day sales cycles
  • Committee buyers vs. individual decision-makers
  • Budget-constrained vs. ROI-focused
  • Relationship-driven vs. results-driven

Creator/Course:

  • Aspiration-driven buyers
  • Career changers vs. skill enhancers
  • Preference for self-paced vs. guided learning
  • Community seekers vs. content consumers

Step 4: Persona Scoring Matrix

After building all personas, create a comparison matrix:

## Matriz de Pontuação de Personas

| Persona | Relevância (1-5) | Potencial de Receita | Tamanho do Público | Custo de Aquisição | Prioridade |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Persona 1] | ★★★★★ | Muito Alto | Médio | Moderado | Primária |
| [Persona 2] | ★★★★☆ | Alto | Grande | Fácil | Primária |
| [Persona 3] | ★★★★☆ | Médio | Grande | Fácil | Secundária |
| [Persona 4] | ★★★☆☆ | Alto | Pequeno | Difícil | Secundária |
| [Persona 5] | ★★★☆☆ | Médio | Médio | Moderado | Terciária |
| [Persona 6] | ★★☆☆☆ | Baixo | Grande | Fácil | Terciária |

Scoring criteria:

  • Relevance (1-5): How close is this persona to the business’s ideal customer
  • 5 = Perfect match, highest conversion probability
  • 4 = Strong match, proven buyer profile
  • 3 = Moderate combination, requires nurturing
  • 2 = Weak combination, low expected conversion
  • 1 = Marginal match, target only if the budget allows
  • Revenue Potential: Expected lifetime value of this persona
  • Audience Size: How large is this segment on ad platforms
  • Acquisition Cost: Estimated relative cost to acquire this persona
  • Priority: Primary (target first), Secondary (expand to), Tertiary (test with the remaining budget)
