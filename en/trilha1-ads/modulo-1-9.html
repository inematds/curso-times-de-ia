Assumptions and Important Notices

  • CPM/CPC/CPA estimates based on industry averages through [current year]
  • Actual performance depends on creative quality, landing page conversion rates, and market conditions
  • Projections assume conversion tracking is installed correctly
  • The first 2-4 weeks are a learning phase — expect above-average CPAs
  • The numbers improve as pixel data accumulates and algorithms optimize

Rules

  1. ALWAYS request or estimate AOV/LTV — without it, ROI projections are meaningless
  2. ALWAYS present all 3 scenarios — let the user choose their risk tolerance
  3. ALWAYS calculate the break-even CPA — this is the most important number in the report
  4. ALWAYS use industry-specific benchmarks, not generic averages
  5. ALWAYS include the scaling roadmap — users need to know where to go next
  6. ALWAYS include daily budget numbers, not just monthly figures — media buyers work with daily budgets
  7. NEVER recommend LinkedIn if the monthly budget is under $1,000—CPCs make it unviable
  8. NEVER recommend more than 2 platforms if the budget is under $2,000—too much spread
  9. NEVER project ROAS without stating assumptions—always note that projections are estimates
  10. NEVER ignore minimum viable budget thresholds—some platforms need minimum spend to optimize
  11. ALWAYS flag when a budget is too low for a platform (“$300/month on LinkedIn won’t generate meaningful data”)
  12. Full budget plan output for ADS-BUDGET.md in the current working directory
