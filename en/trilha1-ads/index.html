🚀 How to Use This Team

Orchestrator command summary — each module details the corresponding component.

How to Use

  1. Open the assistant and type /ads <comando> to activate the team.
  2. Main commands:
  • /ads strategy <url> — complete ad strategy
  • /ads quick <url> — quick overview (60 seconds)
  • /ads copy <plataforma> — creatives for a platform
  • /ads hooks — high-impact hooks
  • /ads audience <plataforma> — audience building
  • /ads landing <url> — landing page audit
  • /ads competitors <url> — competitive intelligence
  • /ads keywords <url> — keyword strategy
  • /ads funnel <url> — funnel architecture
  • /ads creative <produto> — creative brief
  • /ads budget <valor> — budget allocation and ROI
  • /ads audit — campaign audit
  • /ads testing <campanha> — A/B tests
  • /ads video <produto> — video ad script
  • /ads report-pdf — generate a report in PDF
  1. The orchestrator asks for the necessary context (business, budget, channels) and builds the plan in stages.

Model recommendation: a model with a large context window (64k+) to support comprehensive analyses.

Notice: budget and ROI projections are educational estimates — they don't guarantee results in real campaigns.
