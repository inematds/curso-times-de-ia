Skill Purpose

Analyze the performance of existing ad campaigns based on user-provided data (screenshots, descriptions, or metrics). Evaluate key performance indicators against industry benchmarks, detect creative fatigue and audience overlap, identify wasted budget, and provide prioritized optimization recommendations ranked by expected impact. This is a diagnostic tool—it shows what’s working, what’s broken, and what should be fixed first.

When to Use

  • The user wants to audit their existing ad campaigns
  • The user shares ad performance data (screenshots, CSV, or described metrics)
  • The user asks "why aren't my ads working?" or "how can I improve my ads?"
  • The user wants to know whether their ad spend is efficient
  • The user has been running ads for 7+ days and wants a performance check
  • Activated by /ads audit or /ads audit <plataforma>

Adjustments by Industry Vertical:

  • E-commerce: CPA benchmarks $15-$45, ROAS target 3-5x
  • SaaS/B2B: CPA benchmarks $50-$200, focus on CPL rather than CPA
  • Local Services: CPA benchmarks $20-$75, call tracking is critical
  • Education/Courses: CPA benchmarks $30-$100, focus on webinar registrations
  • Real estate: CPL benchmarks $20-$80, lead quality matters more than volume

Stage 3: Performance Analysis

Run each of these diagnostic checks:

3A: Efficiency Analysis

Calculate and evaluate:

  • Cost Efficiency Score: How much value per dollar spent?
  • Click Efficiency: CTR vs. benchmark for this platform/industry
  • Conversion Efficiency: Conversion rate vs. benchmark
  • ROAS Check: Is the return justifying the spend?
  • Budget Utilization: Is the daily budget being fully spent? Underspending signals targeting that is too narrow.

3B: Creative Fatigue Detection

Identify whether the ads are fatigued:

SignalLimitStatus
CTR drop>20% drop in 7 daysFatigued
Frequency>3 in 7 daysOversaturation
CPC increase>30% increase in 7 daysAuction pressure
Relevance/Quality DecliningDeclining scoreObsolete creative
Declining conversion rate>15% dropMessage exhausted

Creative Fatigue Diagnosis:

  • Light fatigue: CTR fell 10-20%, frequency 2-3 → Update the ad copy and images
  • Moderate fatigue: CTR fell 20-40%, frequency 3-5 → New creative concepts needed
  • Severe fatigue: CTR fell >40%, frequency >5 → Pause and relaunch with completely new creative

3C: Audience Analysis

Evaluate targeting effectiveness:

  • Audience Size: Too broad (>10M on Meta) or too narrow (<100K)?
  • Audience overlap: Multiple ad sets targeting the same people?
  • Demographic Performance: Which age/gender/location segments convert best?
  • Placement Performance: Which placements (feed, stories, reels, search, display) perform best?
  • Performance by Device: Conversion differences between mobile and desktop?

3D: Budget Waste Identification

Find where money is being wasted:

Waste TypeHow to DetectCorrection

Poor PlacementsHigh-spend, low-conversion placementsExclude underperformers

Device MismatchMany mobile clicks, but desktop-only landing pageOptimize LP for mobile

Calculate the drop at each stage:

  • Impression to Click: Is the ad compelling? (Creative issue)
  • Click to Preview Landing Page: Is the page loading? (Technical issue)
  • Landing Page Conversion Review: Is the offer converting? (Offer/UX problem)

Step 4: Scoring

Overall Ad Performance Score (0-100):

DimensionWeightScore RangeRating
Cost Efficiency25%0-25CPC, CPM, CPA vs benchmarks
Creative Performance20%0-20CTR, engagement, fatigue level
Conversion Quality25%0-25Conversion rate, ROAS, lead quality
Audience Targeting15%0-15Targeting accuracy, overlap, segmentation
Budget Optimization15%0-15Budget utilization, waste, allocation

Score Interpretation:

  • 80-100: Excellent — only minor optimizations
  • 60-79: Good — solid foundation with opportunities for optimization
  • 40-59: Needs Work — significant improvements needed in several areas
  • 20-39: Poor — fundamental strategy problems to resolve
  • 0-19: Critical — serious issues, consider pausing and restructuring

Step 5: Generate Recommendations

Produce recommendations at 3 levels, ordered by expected impact:

Level 1: Immediate Actions (This Week) Actions you can take today without additional resources:

  • Pause underperforming ad sets
  • Adjust budgets for top performers
  • Add negative keywords (Google)
  • Adjust frequency caps
  • Fix audience overlap
  • Update ad copy with better-performing hooks

Level 2: Short-Term Optimizations (Next 2 Weeks) Actions that require some creative or strategic work:

  • Launch new creative variations
  • Test new audiences
  • A/B test landing pages
  • Implement remarketing
  • Adjust bidding strategy
  • Expand to new placements

Level 3: Strategic Changes (Next 30 Days) Actions that require significant planning and effort:

  • Restructure campaign architecture
  • Build new funnel stages
  • Create new creative concepts
  • Expand to new platforms
  • Redesign landing pages
  • Implement conversion tracking improvements

Important Rules

  • Never make assumptions about missing data — request it or flag it as unavailable
  • Always compare with platform-specific benchmarks, not averages across platforms
  • The vertical matters—adjust the benchmarks accordingly
  • Frequency is one of the most overlooked metrics — always check it
  • ROAS alone is not enough—consider contribution margin and LTV
  • Identifying budget waste should always include estimated dollar amounts
  • Recommendations should be specific and actionable, not generic best practices
  • Tier 1 recommendations should be feasible without requiring new creative assets
  • Always account for seasonality when evaluating performance trends
  • If the data is insufficient for a full audit, state that clearly and specify what's needed
  • A declining CTR with stable conversions may be acceptable — context matters
  • Never recommend increasing a campaign budget if there are fundamental creative or targeting issues
